Why This Matters

AI agents are starting to access real tools: repositories, documents, messaging platforms, internal APIs, and business systems. Without governance, this creates security, compliance, and visibility risks.

Access Risk

Agents may get access to too many tools or overly broad permissions.

Credential Risk

Static secrets and shared credentials make tool access difficult to control.

Audit Risk

Teams need visibility into what tools were called, by whom, and why.

Agent Sentinel is not a claim that every component is proprietary or built from scratch. It is a service offering that brings together open-source tools, public MCP security patterns, and implementation guidance into a usable enterprise approach.

The value is in selecting the right components, integrating them correctly, hardening the deployment, and helping teams operate them safely.

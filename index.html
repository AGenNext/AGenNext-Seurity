Why This Matters

AI systems are no longer just chat interfaces. They connect to tools, retrieve data, invoke APIs, write code, and make workflow decisions. That creates new security risks across identity, data, tools, memory, supply chain, and governance.

Agentic Risk

Agents can misuse tools, follow malicious instructions, or take actions outside the intended workflow.

Data Risk

Sensitive information can leak through prompts, retrieval systems, logs, outputs, or connected tools.

Supply Chain Risk

Models, plugins, MCP servers, prompts, packages, and datasets can introduce hidden or inherited vulnerabilities.
